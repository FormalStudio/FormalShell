# Activity on screen, and the glass

Phase 3 of `Plan.md` (decisions 5, 6, 9 and 12). 3.2 (FormalSpec's
operation stream, its `docs/activity.md` v1) and 3.3's data half
(`crates/fs-activity`, `services/activity.rs`, the `--activity` leg) are
done. This spec is the rest: what Activity looks like and does (3.3), and
the translucent fills with the layerrules they need (3.1). Where it
disagrees with the 2026-10-29 one-look spec, this one wins for Activity and
the glass; the one-look spec still owns the table.

## 1. One row, four places

Everything Activity draws is built from one element, `activity::row`
(`surfaces/activity.rs`): a ring, the group's title, one line of detail and
its actions. The bar cell, the live toast, the Activity panel and the
notification centre all use it, and phase 4's sidebar card is
`activity::list(.., max 3)` with no new view code.

A group is fs-activity's `Group`: every item sharing a `source` (one apply
host, one update spec, one app). The row shows the group's `lead` (a
failure, else the oldest running item, else the last to end) and, past one
item, a count.

| Part | Running | Succeeded / done | Failed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ring | `track.fill` arc from twelve o'clock over a `track.groove` circle; no progress is a quarter arc turning once every two `PULSE_MS` under the motion scale, still where motion is off | full ring, `check` inside | `destructive` ring, `x` inside |
| title | fspec's `title`, the app's name, a notification's summary | same | same |
| detail | `item`, else the phase by name; progress as `3 of 12 resources` or `40%` (mono) | `result.summary` | `error`, two lines |
| meta | `x2` past one item, the source mark (`package` for fspec, the app's icon) | | |

The ring is a new kit element, `w::ring(progress, state)` (`Kind::Ring`),
`controlHeight` across, its stroke `xs`, the same proportions the headset
card's battery rings draw by hand (they move onto it). A turning ring asks
its host for frames (`cx.animate()`) and damages its own square only.

## 2. Actions

| Action | fspec | LauncherEntry | notification |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| open (click, Enter) | opens the Activity panel on that row | focuses the app's window, else launches its entry | the notification's default action |
| dismiss (X, `x`) | a running group's toast goes until its state changes; a finished or failed group folds into the history now (`seen` for that source) | same | same, and the notification closes |
| retry (`r`, the row's button) | `run`: `fspec run --detach <package>`; `update`: `fspec update` in the spec dir off its source; `apply` and `rollback` carry no spec path or argv in stream v1, so they have no retry | none | none |

Retry spawns fspec as a detached child on the service thread; its new
operation arrives on the stream like any other, so the row needs no
optimistic state. IPC grows `activity dismiss <source>`, `activity retry
<source>` and `activity open <source>`, each an error string for a source
nothing holds, recorded in `ipc-golden.jsonl`.

## 3. The bar cell

The first cell of the Indicators rail (the loud ones lead), so every layout
carrying `indicators` has it with no setting; the cell name `activity` is
reserved for a standalone entry later and is not added now. It exists only
while `live` holds a group: at idle it is `View::hidden`, measured once,
and never commits.

While shown it is a self-drawn cell: the lead group's ring at the icon's
size, then its title as a free label at the room `bar room` hands it
(capped at `labelCap`, the window title's ceiling), then `+N` in mono for
the other groups. A failure takes the `destructive` tone and leads; a
success shows the check for its four seconds. The tooltip is the lead's
title and detail. Click opens the `activity` panel from the cell. A vertical
bar shows the ring and the count alone.

Cost: a determinate ring repaints only when a stream line changes it (fspec
coalesces to 50 ms); a turning ring is the only thing animating, and only
while the lead has no progress.

## 4. The live toast

One card per group in the sonner stack, keyed `activity:<source>`, for the
fspec and LauncherEntry origins. A notification carrying a `value` hint is
already its own live toast (it replaces in place through `replaces_id` and
`x-canonical-private-synchronous`); its bubble draws the same ring and
progress line instead of raising a second card, so every origin looks the
same and none appears twice.

The card is the bubble's chrome with `activity::row` inside it, ranked in
the stack by the group's last change; a failed group ranks as critical. It
shows while the group is live and not dismissed, so it leaves with the fold
four seconds after success. Do Not Disturb holds running cards back and
still lets a failure through, the way critical notifications pass it. The
card never times out on its own while running: an hour's apply keeps one
card, updated in place, until it ends or is dismissed. A click on its body
is open, its X is dismiss.

## 5. The list

**The Activity panel** (`panel open activity`, the cell's panel): a
`Now` section of every live group, then `Earlier` (the history, newest
first, ten rows), each row a stop. Enter opens, `x` dismisses, `r`
retries. Nothing live and no history is one dim line, `Nothing running`.

**The notification centre** gains an `Activity` section over its pending
tier while the history holds anything: the folded rows, newest first, five
at most, and a `Clear` that empties the history. This is where finished
work folds to (decision 12).

**Honest unavailable.** fspec not on PATH: the panel's last line reads
`fspec is not installed: installs and updates do not show here`, and
LauncherEntry and notification progress still show. fspec present but its
directory unreadable: the service's error, verbatim. No stub rows, ever.

## 6. Glass (3.1)

Starts after the phase 1.4 agent ships 1.4c, which owns the table's roles
until then.

- The shell asks the compositor whether it blurs (Hyprland
  `decoration:blur:enabled` over `hyprctl getoption -j`, re-read on config
  reload; mango never blurs) and publishes it in `debug dump` as `blur`.
- Where it blurs, `theme.surfaceOpacity` defaults below 1 and the toast's
  `notification` role takes the same translucent `card` fill; where it does
  not, every card draws opaque whatever the key says, so text never sits on
  a busy wallpaper (decision 9).
- fspec's `home/.config/hypr/` ships the layerrules: `blur` and
  `ignore_alpha` per shell namespace that draws a translucent card (bar,
  panel, launcher, notifications), none for tooltips, menus and xdg popups,
  and no window blur on popups.
- The opacity and `ignore_alpha` are tuned together on g815's real GPU, read
  off `grim` photographs of the live screen after a ship; the shell is
  never driven there.

## 7. Verification

`--activity` grows: the cell, the toast and the panel photographed while
the stub's apply is at 1/4 and while the update has failed, the centre's
section after the fold, then the same under `--light`. It asserts what it
can read: `activity status`'s `toasts` (the groups with a card),
`notifications status` holding no second card for the `value` notification,
the cell's rect in `bar room`, retry of a stubbed `run` reaching a
PATH-shimmed `fspec` with exactly `run --detach <pkg>`, and ten stub
operations at once leaving one cell with `+9`, three toasts at most fanned
and every row in the panel (the overflow case). `--idle` and
`--menu-budget` under `FS_CPU_QUOTA` hold the phase 1 gate with the cell in
the rail.
