# The sidebar

Phase 4 of `Plan.md` (decisions 2, 5 and 12). `layout: "sidebar"` puts the
shell's chrome in a column on a side edge instead of a strip: compact
cards, a Spaces minimap you can act on, a status card at the foot. Where it
disagrees with the 2026-10-29 one-look spec, this one wins for the
sidebar; the table still owns every number it does not name here.

The composition and its lengths are Pond's sidebar (BSD-3-Clause,
Butterfly Interaction; `pond/src/shell/Theme.qml`, `Sidebar.qml`,
`calendar/Clock.qml`, `minimap/Map.qml`). Pond's card is 156 wide; ours is
136, the proportion the owner's reference shot keeps on a 1920 output, so
every Pond length is taken times 136/156 and rounded (`sidebar::Geo`,
which grows with `sidebarWidth`). Colours, fonts, the card's fill, border
and radius stay the table's.

## 1. One bar, two bodies

The sidebar is the bar. `Bar` already lives on a vertical edge, one per
output (`wayland/heads.rs`), reserves its edge with an exclusive zone,
hides under a fullscreen window and opens a gap in its own line for
every card joined to it. Under `layout: "sidebar"` it keeps all of that
and swaps its body: no cells and no strip, but a column of cards drawn by
the ui kit (`ui::Ui::draw` over an `El` tree from `surfaces/sidebar/`).

| Key | Under the sidebar |
| --- | --- |
| `bar.position` | `left` or `right` place it; `top` and `bottom` put it on the left (Plan.md decision 2) |
| `bar.style` | the band's paint as on a vertical bar: `transparent` reads its ink off the wallpaper, `solid`, `corners` with their fillets |
| `bar.layout`, `bar.modules` | not read: the sidebar's cards are fixed |
| `frame.thickness` | not drawn: the sidebar and the frame do not combine, and the dump says so (`frame.ignored`) |
| `fullscreen.hideChrome` | hides it as it hides the bar |

Width: `sidebarWidth` in the table, 154: a 9px margin, the 136 card and
9 more, the zone leaving Hyprland's `gaps_out` to the compositor so the
windows sit 9 off the cards as the cards sit off the screen. The bar's `thickness()` is that
width, so a card hanging off the sidebar finds its line where it finds the
bar's today. Collapsed it is the rail: `sidebarRail`, 48, one icon button
across with the band's padding either side.

## 2. The cards

Top to bottom, each a `card` box as wide as the column less the 9 margin
either side, 5 apart, as tall as the lengths below:

| Card | What it holds | Missing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Time and date | 50 tall: the time in the desktop clock's face (`desktopClock.face`, `width` and `color`, its frosted glass over the wallpaper under the card, and the subject its depth map cuts laid back over the digits), its figures 24 tall at most and 10 in, the grid faces' hours and minutes set as one run the way the desktop clock sets them, the serif face's with its colon; `auto` ink is light or dark off the card behind the digits; the format's AM/PM after it at 10, medium, 0.7 opacity, on its baseline (a format with no AM/PM is 24h and has none); at the far end, 10 in, a 35 box (radius 5, the outline hairline) holding the weekday over the day at 11. A format wider than the room shrinks the digits rather than cut them. Painted over a reserved box, since the type scale has none of those sizes; a click opens the calendar | never missing |
| Spaces | the minimap (section 4), 9 inside the card | never missing: an output with no workspace shows its one empty row |
| Now playing | 54 tall: the 33 art (a click opens the media panel), the title in semibold over the artist muted, both elided | no card without a player |
| Notifications | 42 tall, 14 in: the bell (its DND mark), the count (unseen popups and pending, else every entry) medium and muted, a 1 x 12 rule, then the distinct apps of the latest at 12, the first whole and the rest at 0.4, as many as fit; a click opens the centre | `Notifications` muted, the card kept |
| Activity | `activity::list(.., 3)`, the same rows the panel draws (decision 12: three at most, a `+n` line past them) | no card while nothing runs or waits |
| Software | the spec checkout (`FSPEC_SPEC`) against its upstream, fetched when first drawn and every half hour: `n updates` over the upstream's name with Apply (fast-forward, then `fspec apply --rev HEAD`), and the newest `feat(apps): install|uninstall <name> on <this host>` commit as `Installed` or `Removed` over the app's name with Undo (`fspec app undo --detach`); `packages check|apply|undo` over IPC | `Updates need fspec` with no fspec on PATH, `Up to date`, or why it cannot tell (no `FSPEC_SPEC`, no upstream), the card kept |

The status card is pinned to the foot, 42 tall: network, audio, settings,
the tray while it has items, and collapse, muted icons sharing its width,
each with its tooltip. Network and audio open their panels, settings
opens the launcher on its `system` route, the tray button opens the
tray's second bar (`trayoverflow`) hanging off the column beside it,
where a click activates an item and a right click opens its menu, and
collapse folds the column to the rail.

A card past the column's height is the last thing to go: Now playing
drops first, then Software, then Activity, then Notifications; Time and
Spaces always stay, the minimap then showing the rows that fit (the
cursor's, a drag's target or a newly current row brought into view) and a
wheel over it moving them a row a notch.

The rail keeps the clock as `HH` over `MM` in mono, the workspace
ordinals as a column of chips (the focused one in `primary`), the Activity
ring when something runs, and the status card's buttons stacked (network,
audio, settings, expand). The expand button is the rail's last.

Collapse state is runtime state: `sidebarCollapsed` in state.json, never
settings.json. `sidebar collapse|expand|toggle|status` over IPC; `status`
reports `collapsed`, `width`, `edge`, the cards drawn, per minimap row
its workspace id and window ids in order, and every control's rect in the
window (`hits`), which the legs click.

## 3. Buds

Panels and the launcher bud off the sidebar's inner edge with the bar's
own join (`fs-chrome` drawer, `Card::new` off `bar.edge()`, `length()`
and `thickness()`), anchored on the card or button that opened them. A
panel from the status row hangs off the line beside that button; the
launcher from the settings button or `menu toggle` rests centred both
ways in the area the column leaves. `motion.morph: false` gives the popover rise as it does
for the bar. The launcher's own code (`surfaces/launcher.rs`, `fs-menu`)
is the launcher agent's: anything there changes through it.

## 4. The minimap

Each workspace on the sidebar's output is a row of its windows, three
across, each a 35 circle with the outline hairline and its icon at 14,
left to right in layout order (each window's rect, x then y, ties by id),
wrapping to more lines when it holds more than three. Empty slots are 4px
dots in muted ink at 0.3; the current row, when empty, rings its first.
Circles and lines are 6 apart. One empty row sits below the last, for a
new workspace, and dots fill out the map to three rows. The current
workspace's row stands on a plate in the cell's `selected` fill, 4 in
from the card's side and half a gap past the circles. There are no
ordinals at rest: each row's number shows on a 12 x 17 pill straddling
the card's edge while the pointer is on the map or an icon is lifted.
Icons come from compositor metadata through `appicon` exactly as the
Spaces cell's do; nothing is captured (the capture rule).

A click on an icon focuses that window; a click on a row's empty part
focuses that workspace.

### Moves

| Gesture | Keys | Command |
| --- | --- | --- |
| drag an icon along its row | Shift + Left or Right | under the scrolling layout `MoveColumn { id, before }`, its column moved to the drop slot; under any other `SwapWindows { a, b }` with the window at the drop slot |
| drag an icon to another row | Shift + Up or Down | `MoveWindowTo { id, idx, output }`, then a swap into the drop slot when one was named |
| drop on the empty row | Shift + Down past the last row | `MoveWindowTo` the next free ordinal |

Hyprland: `hl.dsp.window.swap({ window = a, target = b })` and the
existing `hl.dsp.window.move`. Under the scrolling layout (a workspace's
`tiledLayout`, `Workspace::columns`) a move along a row moves the column,
as Plan.md asks. The scrolling layout has no move-column-to-index in 0.56
or 0.57 (`CScrollingAlgorithm::layoutMsg`: `move`, `colresize`, `fit`,
`focus`, `promote`, `swapcol`, ...), only `swapcol l|r`, which trades the
focused column with its neighbour; `MoveColumn` is one `hyprctl eval`
chunk that finds the columns by their windows' x, focuses the window and
walks its column to the slot one `hl.dsp.layout("swapcol ..")` at a time,
the columns between shifting one along. A drop on another row under it
moves the window there, then its column into the slot. mango:
`MoveWindowTo` is its `move_to`; a swap and a column move are logged
no-ops there.

The keyboard reaches the sidebar through `sidebar focus` (a keybind's
target): the layer takes the keyboard exclusively, the arrows move a
cursor over the icons (`ring`, DESIGN.md §1), Shift with an arrow carries
the window under the cursor (past the last row only from a row it does not
leave empty), Enter focuses what the cursor is on, and Enter or Escape
gives the keyboard back. Hyprland raises no event for a swap, so the
backend asks for the windows again after one.

### Motion

A press past a 6px threshold lifts the icon, Pond's way: its circle
filled in `popover` with a `primary` ring over a `shadow` disc dropped
down and to the right, the icon 1.15 times its size. Its siblings slide
apart live to show the drop slot (a `muted` circle), the target row takes
the plate, and the icon follows the pointer one to one. Pond's niri also
opens its overview for the drag; the Hyprland side does not.
On release it settles into its slot on the spatial clock, and the map
holds that predicted order until the compositor's snapshot agrees or
`HOLD_MS` (600) passes. A snapshot that disagrees at the deadline, or a
refused command, lets the prediction go and every icon glides back to
where the model has it: the spring-back. The windows move on the
compositor's own animation at the same moment, so the map and the screen
move together. A release outside the sidebar cancels the drag with the
same glide.

## 5. Verification

New legs, each with its header, its smoke-map entry and its CLAUDE.md
line in the commit that adds it:

- `--sidebar`: `layout: sidebar` from settings.json; one `formalshell:bar`
  layer per output 154 wide on the left with the zone reserved, each card
  read off `sidebar status` and the frame, the foot row's buttons opening
  their panels, collapse to 48 with state.json carrying it and back,
  `bar.position: right` moving it, read dark and light; the clock card
  over a staged wallpaper and depth map read off `debug dump`'s
  `sidebarClock`: frosted digits following the wallpaper under them, the
  subject over them, the date box drawn, the bar clock's own time and
  period, and no commit in 3s at rest. Ridden by `--tray`
  or `--tray-overflow` it pins the layout alone and those legs reach the
  tray through the status card's button.
- `--sidebar-join`: the network panel and the launcher budding off the
  inner edge, sampled frame by frame under `debug motionScale`: the first
  frame on the edge, a frame part way out still on it, the panel resting
  against it and the launcher travelling on to rest centred in the area
  the column leaves, read dark and light. The default transparent column draws no line, so no gap is read.
- `--minimap`: three foot windows over two workspaces; rows and order off
  `sidebar status` against `hyprctl clients -j`; a click focusing; a
  pointer drag along a row swapping two windows, one to the other row
  moving it, one to the empty row making workspace 3, each read back off
  `hyprctl clients`; the same three over `sidebar focus` and Shift with
  arrows; a drag let go past the column moving nothing and holding no order;
  a mid-drag frame and status showing the lifted icon, the gap it opens
  and the lit row. A refused command takes the same glide back as the
  cancel once `HOLD_MS` passes; the unit tests hold the drop's commands
  and predicted order. Last the scrolling layout, three windows on
  workspace 4: a drag of the first past the last moving its column to the
  tape's end and Shift+Left carrying it back one, read off the windows' x
  order, with a mid-drag frame and one after.

`--idle` and `--menu-budget` under `FS_CPU_QUOTA` on g815 with the sidebar
on, against the bar's numbers: no commit at rest past the clock's minute.
